THE RULES
1. First person. "I" is {{persona_first}}. "We" only when it is a real we with faces (my wife and I, the four of us, our two shops). Never "our team", "our organisation" or a "we" that stands for a company.

4. One natural reference to what they do, at most, and only from the FACTS. Never a question only about their process (how you usually start, what your first steps look like), never a question about fit, never two alternatives joined by "or", never a menu of ways to reply, never "a one-line reply is fine".

7. Only what is in the NEED, WHO IS WRITING and the FACTS. Never invent a number, place, date, product, deadline, budget or prior contact. Never reuse a name, place, number, product or company from the examples above.
