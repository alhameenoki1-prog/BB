Worker

Your personal copy of the engine. Every cycle (1, 2 or 5 minutes, set by the speed on your profile) it writes emails for new recipients, sends what is due inside your sending hours and daily limit, and reads replies. Saving your profile or AI provider restarts a running worker so the change applies at once.
