Email and password any mailbox, limited mode

Works with Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, Zoho, Fastmail and custom domains. The servers are worked out from the address. Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud want an app password. Limited mode means the mailbox is polled rather than pushed, so new mail shows up within a couple of minutes instead of seconds. The password is stored encrypted.

Set the servers myself

Or paste the whole login at once

Paste the mailbox's settings as you have them and the site reads the address, password and servers out of it: the settings block a provider shows, a imap://… or smtp://… link, a little JSON, or just you@domain.com  app-password on one line. Missing servers are worked out from the address.

Add several at once

One account per line: the address and its password (an app password for Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud), separated by a comma, a tab or a space. The servers are worked out from each address; add them after the password (smtp.host:587, imap.host:993) for a mailbox they cannot be worked out for. Each is tested on its own, and one that fails does not stop the rest.

Several mailboxes of one Microsoft 365 tenant through the same app registration (the Microsoft 365 app card below explains the app): the app's ids once, then one address per line.

Google and Microsoft sign-ins are one browser sign-in each: use the buttons below once per account (each opens that account's settings afterwards; come back here for the next).

Microsoft 365 app app registration, full mode, no sign-in per user

For a mailbox on Microsoft 365: an admin registers an app in Entra (Azure AD), grants it the Mail.ReadWrite and Mail.Send application permissions with admin consent, and creates a client secret. Reads and sends go through the Graph API as that mailbox; replies keep Outlook's conversation. The secret is stored encrypted.
